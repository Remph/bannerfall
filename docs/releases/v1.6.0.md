**Bannerfall is feature complete.** 1.6 brings the last of the features the game was planned with: morale, a rising price for nobles, archers, demolishing buildings, village automation, NPC lords that learn and help their own villages, map sizes, starting next to a friend and a guided setup for the host. What comes after this is polish and nice-to-haves. The release builds on 1.5.1; existing worlds keep running and update their database by themselves (migrations V49 to V61).

**Heads-up for running worlds:** the update switches on three rules in every world, the running ones included: **morale** (floor 30 %), the **rising noble price** (+25 % per village and noble beyond four) and **guild mates starting together**. NPC lords on *Normal* now farm up to **0.75** of their daily production instead of 0.5, and lords send their surplus to their own villages. Village automation is open from a player's third village. Archers and learning lords stay off until the host switches them on. Every setting is on the admin page's World tab; the host can switch each of them off there.

## What Bannerfall is

A browser strategy game for a small group of friends, in German and English, built for phones first and just as usable on a desktop. One world runs for weeks or for good:

- **Villages:** 16 buildings, from the Town Hall to the Banner Hall, a village picture that grows with them, notes per village, and now demolishing and automatic building plans
- **Troops:** spearmen, swordsmen, axemen, scouts, light and heavy cavalry, rams, catapults, nobles, the paladin, the banner thief and, optionally, archers and mounted archers; unit levels researched in the Smithy; recruit templates; dismissing units
- **War:** attacks, supports, attack trains, scouting, a wall, the night bonus, loyalty and conquest, morale, an attack simulator and detailed battle reports to share
- **Banners:** found in battle, flown from the Banner Hall, fused, stolen by banner thieves and carried as the paladin's standards
- **Economy:** resources and storage, a market with offers and exchange, merchants between villages, a farm list with templates and "Send all"
- **Players together:** guilds with chat, a political map and shared reports; private messages; world chat; achievements; statistics with ranking tabs and charts over time
- **NPC lords and barbarians:** computer lords that build, farm, scout, conquer, form guilds, fly banners and now learn from players, so a world with only a few people still has opponents; barbarian villages to farm, with walls
- **How a world ends:** domination, banner keeps or an end date, with a victory clock
- **Comfort:** push notifications while the game is closed, five colour themes in light and dark, medieval or classic icons, a live map with movement lines
- **For hosts:** one Docker image, a guided setup, the admin page and the `bannerfall-admin` console, update and backup scripts, several worlds on one server, and an optional login through an account server shared by several worlds

## What is new

**Morale**
- An attack on a much smaller player hits weaker: with the defender's points under about a quarter of the attacker's, the attack loses strength down to the world's floor (30 % by default). The curve is the classic `0.3 + 3 × defender's points / attacker's points`, read when the attack arrives
- Only the attacker's strength changes. Defence always stays at 100 %, and a smaller player attacking a bigger one never gets more than 100 %
- Players and NPC lords alike, both ways. Barbarian villages have no morale
- Shown before sending (the send sheet, the map's farm line and the farm list's rows say "Morale 48 %") and afterwards in both battle reports. The simulator has a morale field

**Fairer noble growth**
- Each noble gets dearer the more a player already has: the price is the base × (1 + 0.25 × rank), at most 7×, where the rank is villages + nobles − 4. A player with one village still pays the base price for the first four nobles; with three nobles in hand, five villages pay 2× and ten 3.25×
- Nobles count wherever they are (home, on the way, in training). A conquest leaves the rank as it was; a lost village or a dead noble lowers it. Several nobles in one order pay their average, so ordering at once is never cheaper
- NPC lords pay the same price. The noble's row in the Academy has an info button with the price, the rank and the next step. The host sets the step (0 = the flat price of before)

**Archers** (optional, off by default)
- The archer (Barracks) is a cheap defender, weak only against other archers; the mounted archer (Stable) is a fast raider. Both have their own attack class: every unit now has a defence against archers (spearmen are weak against them, heavy cavalry very strong)
- Smithy levels, templates, the simulator and NPC lords' mixes know them. A world without archers fights exactly as before

**Demolishing buildings**
- The Town Hall has an "Upgrade | Demolish" switch. A demolition takes one level, runs in the build queue and takes a fifth of that level's build time. It costs nothing and gives nothing back
- Town Hall, farm and warehouse stay at level 1 at least. The list names what a demolition would lock (buildings or units that need the level) before you confirm. A farm can't shrink below the population in use

**Village automation** (from your third village)
- Up to five build plans (A to E): ordered lines "building to level N" and, if you like, one line "start recruit template X". Pick a plan per village and it builds by itself, also while you are offline, at normal prices and in the normal queue places (never a paid slot, at most two of its own orders)
- A full warehouse or farm comes first, levels you build by hand count, losses to catapults are built again, and the recruit line keeps a template filled. Nobles and banner thieves are never recruited automatically
- Cancelling one of its orders, or demolishing a level the plan builds, pauses it. A cogwheel marks its orders. The host sets from which village on it opens (0 = off)

**Playing together**
- **Start next to a friend:** in a world with guilds, a player whose guild already has a member with a village starts 4 to 6 fields from that member at the opening, and the respawn screen offers "Start next to my guild". The guild is the agreement. The host can switch it off
- The NPC lords' guild brake now compares points per member, so two friends in one guild aren't braked as if they were a big guild

**NPC lords**
- **Learning lords** (off by default): lords adapt their troop mix and wall to the attacks on their own villages and to the anonymous mix of attacks that win for human players, by at most 35 % of their mix and fading over a real day
- **Surplus shipments** (on by default): what would overflow a lord's warehouse goes by merchant to its own villages that still build
- A lord with an Academy picks a conquest target only after scouting shows it can win, so an offensive lord no longer stalls at one village with its army spent on nobles
- *Normal* lords farm up to 0.75 of their daily production per day instead of 0.5

**Map sizes**
- The host picks how far apart the sides start: Close, Normal, Wide or Vast (factor 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.3 / 1.6). It scales the spawn rings, the barbarian and keep rings and the lords' radii, and brings its own map size and noble range. Normal is the behaviour of before; the world info says which one a world uses

**Farm list**
- Compact rows that open on a tap. Each entry shows the most one attack can bring home (carry capacity) next to the last raid's haul, e.g. "632/800"
- The first tap of "Send all" or "Send banner chances" says what the run would send: "30 attacks, loot up to 22,200"

**Push notifications**
- **Attacks are back:** a switch on the send sheet, under "Send all" and on the bell of an outgoing attack sends one notification when the attack, the train or the whole farm run is home, with the loot (on by default)
- **Storage full:** once per village when any resource reaches the warehouse limit, until it drops again (off by default)
- Firefox on Android no longer counts as a new device at every start (the update keeps each player's newest device per push service)

**Look and feel**
- **Colour themes:** Green banner (as before), Royal blue, Black and gold, Purple and Leather and forest, each in light and dark, under "Colours" in the menu
- **Medieval icons:** 44 game icons from game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0, credited in About) with a "Medieval | Classic" switch per device
- About thanks the test players, and the footer and the menus link to the public repository on GitHub
- Fixes from a UI review: plain links no longer show in browser blue, the village switcher fits at 1024 px, clearer morale and noble price lines, the simulator folds on phones, sheet titles stay above scrolled content, a tap on the map can no longer hit a button of the panel that opens under the finger, and long unit names in battle reports no longer break apart on phones

## For hosts

- **Guided setup:** in a new world, register yourself and run `bannerfall-admin setup`. It prints a one-time link (`https://<your domain>/setup?code=…`, 30 minutes, only in the pre-game). Opening it while logged in makes you the admin and starts a wizard: a starting point (Friends' round, Long-running world, Together against the lords, Custom), then basics, pace, map and opponents, rules, victory and the opening. Everything also stays on the admin page and in `bannerfall-admin world set …`. `docs/hosting.md` §9 and the README describe it
- **Hosting guide:** `docs/hosting.md` went through a fresh-eyes pass on a clean Ubuntu 24.04 with the guided setup. It now pulls the release's published image instead of building it on the server (x86-64 only; on an ARM server you still build it, as step 6 says), explains the pre-game and the setup link, and the update section starts with `ops/bannerfall-update`
- **Admin page:** the World tab is now in the wizard's five sections (Basics, Pace, Map and opponents, Rules, Victory and end) with one save bar; rarely used numbers sit behind "More settings"
- **Thirteen database migrations**, run by Flyway on the first start. They add columns with defaults, nullable columns, tables and one partial index, so they take moments:
  - V49: removes duplicate push devices (each player keeps the newest per push service)
  - V50: `npc_learning_enabled` (false) and an index on attack reports
  - V51: `guild_start_together` (true)
  - V52: `noble_price_step` (0.25)
  - V53: `setup_done` (false; only the pre-game reads it)
  - V54: `archers_enabled` (false) and the two archer columns on the five tables with unit counts
  - V55: `npc_shipments_enabled` (true)
  - V56: `build_order.demolish`
  - V57: `map_spread` (`NORMAL`)
  - V58: tables `build_plan` and `village_automation`, `build_order.automated`, `automation_villages` (3)
  - V59: `morale_enabled` (true) and `morale_floor` (30)
  - V60: tables `return_watch` and `storage_full_push`, `movement.return_watch_id`
  - V61: the last raid's columns on `farm_target`
- **New world settings**, also in the console (`bannerfall-admin world set …`): `morale-enabled`, `morale-floor`, `noble-price-step`, `archers-enabled`, `map-spread` (pre-game only), `guild-start-together`, `npc-learning-enabled`, `npc-shipments-enabled`, `automation-villages`
- **Environment:** `compose.prod.yaml` now passes `BANNERFALL_DOMAIN` (already in every `.env`) to the app, which prints the setup link with it. No other new variables, no change to the world's `Caddyfile`, `edge/` or `ops/`
- API: `POST /api/admin/setup-code`, `POST /api/me/setup-claim`, `POST /api/admin/world-setup/done`; `POST /api/villages/{id}/demolitions`; `GET /api/noble`; the build plan and automation endpoints; the respawn body `{nearGuild}`; reports and send previews carry `morale`; farm entries carry `haulCapacity`, `lastRaid` and `morale`. Details in the API docs

## Upgrading a running world (from v1.5.0 or v1.5.1)

1. `git fetch --tags origin && git checkout v1.6.0`
2. `ops/bannerfall-update 1.6.0` (add `--build` if you build your images on the server). It takes the backup, warns the players, restarts a minute later, applies V49 to V61 and checks the new version (schema 61). Only the app is recreated
3. Look over the admin page's World tab: morale, the noble price, guild mates starting together, lord shipments and the automation are on; turn off what your world shouldn't have
4. Several worlds behind the shared Caddy: update every world, then check out the same tag in the edge folder (`edge/` itself is unchanged)

Or by hand, as before: announce the update (`bannerfall-admin broadcast "Update in 5 minutes"`), take a backup (`ops/bannerfall-backup`), pull the image below or build it with `--build-arg VERSION=1.6.0`, set `BANNERFALL_IMAGE` in `.env` to it, `docker compose -f compose.prod.yaml up -d`, then check `bannerfall-admin status` (version 1.6.0) and `bannerfall-admin check`.

Going back to 1.5.1 means restoring the backup taken before the update and starting the old image: the migrations can't be undone, and the play since the update is lost.
