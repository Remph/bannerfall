A feature release on top of 1.3.2. The paladin moves into the Banner Hall, where he and the banner thief now train their levels and the paladin carries a standard of his own. Farm lists and recruiting get templates, every village can carry a private note, achievements finally reward real fights, and two new world settings (both off by default) let NPC lords use banners and make villages defend twice as strongly at night. It builds on v1.3.2; existing worlds keep running and update their database by themselves (migrations V36 to V43).

**Heads-up for running worlds:** at the first start of 1.4, every paladin whose home village has **no Banner Hall** is dismissed once: at home, in a support, on the way, in a recruit order or in a dismiss order. His home village gets **1,000 wood, clay and iron per paladin back in full**, even above the warehouse capacity. Paladins in villages with a Banner Hall stay. Players get no report about it, so tell them before the update (see *Upgrading a running world*).

## What is new

**The paladin in the Banner Hall**
- The paladin is trained in the **Banner Hall** (needs Banner Hall 1), no longer in the Barracks, and in 3 hours at Banner Hall 1 instead of 6. His slot counter moved with him
- The Banner Hall can be built whenever the world has paladins **or** banners; with banners off it only trains the paladin
- **Hall training:** the hall trains the levels of its own two units, like the Smithy does for the others, and also in worlds with unit levels off. Paladin level 2 and 3 (Banner Hall 2 and 3) give attack 126 / 132 and defence 158 / 165 and cost 5 and 15 times his recruit cost. Banner thief level 2 and 3 raise the theft chance per surviving thief from 3 % to 4 % and 5 %. One training at a time per hall, beside the Smithy's research
- The Banner Hall has its own item in the navigation: a tab after Recruit on desktops, in the village group of the "More" menu on phones. It shows while the world has paladins or banners

**Paladin standards** (with banners and paladins on)
- Seven standards (German "Standarte"), a second family of banners that only paladins carry: Spear and Sword (defence of spearmen or swordsmen, 5 / 10 / 15 %), Axe and Lance (attack of axemen or light cavalry, 5 / 10 / 15 %), Knight (attack and defence of heavy cavalry, 4 / 8 / 12 %), Ram and Siege (rams or catapults count 10 / 20 / 30 % more)
- One standard per village, in its own slot beside the village banner. The village's paladins carry it: into an attack with at least one paladin (it covers that army) and on defence (it covers each defending stack with a paladin). It works up to the owner's paladin level and sleeps without a Banner Hall
- A won battle that the winner's own paladin fought in rolls once more for a standard (the banner drop odds, its own pair window and daily cap). When an attack carrying a standard is beaten, the defender's surviving banner thieves at home may take it (world setting, on by default). Conquest takes it like the village banner
- Standards share the stash and fuse within their own family. The Banner Hall page, battle and scout reports, the attack simulator and the admin page show them

**Farm templates**
- Up to five troop templates **A to E**, shared by all your villages. A farm list entry uses a template or its own units; saving a template changes every entry that uses it, in every village. Deleting one leaves its entries with the same units as their own
- **Farming from the map:** the panel of every village that isn't yours has a farm line, "Farm with [A] [B] …": one tap sends that template as an attack from the current village (the normal rules; two taps when it would end your beginner protection). "Add to farm list" opens a sheet over the map instead of leaving it
- The Rally Point's attack form has the template buttons above the unit counts; a tap fills them in
- Any attack from a village at a target on its farm list now updates that entry's "last sent", however it was sent
- On phones the farm list has two tabs, *Targets | Templates*

**Recruit templates**
- Up to five recruit templates **A to E** with a short name each (up to 16 characters), shared by all your villages. A template is a **goal army**: a tap opens a plan with, per unit, the goal, what the village has (at home, away and in training) and what it recruits now, with the total cost, and one *Recruit* button queues it all
- Short of resources or population, every unit is cut by the same share, so the army grows in the template's proportions. A unit whose goal is reached, whose building is missing or whose queue is full is skipped with the reason; the paladin is capped by the free slots. The edit sheet can take over the village's current army
- The row folds away to one line ("Templates (3)"), and the device remembers it

**Notes per village**
- Write your own note on any village (barbarian, other players', NPC lords' and your own): up to 500 characters, only you can read it. The map panel shows it under the name (without a note, a pencil next to ×), the map marks the village with a small sheet of paper, the desktop tooltip shows its first line, and the farm list shows it as a short line on the row
- A note stays when the village changes owner. It changes nothing in the game

**Night bonus** (world setting, off by default)
- While the host has it on, an attack that **arrives** between 23:00 and 07:00 German time meets **twice the defence** at villages of players and NPC lords, never at barbarian villages. The host sets the hours and the factor (1.1 to 3)
- The send confirmation, the farm list rows and the map's farm line warn when an attack would land at night, attacks under way carry a moon tag, both battle reports say so, the world info lists the window, and the attack simulator has a switch for it (ticked by itself when it is filled from a night report). NPC lords count it in their attack estimates

**Achievements**
- Nine new achievements that only real fights earn: **Slayer** and **Warden** with levels (enemy units killed by your attacks, attackers killed in your villages), and **Pyrrhic Victory**, **Avenger**, **Demolisher**, **Lucky Strike**, **Spy Catcher**, **Last Stand** and **Brothers in Arms**. That makes **40** achievements
- A new category **Defence**, with Shield and Bulwark moved there
- New numbers from a running world: Victor 25 / 250 / 2,500, Growth 1,000 / 5,000 / 30,000, Standing Army 200 / 2,500 / 20,000, Bulwark 3 / 15 / 50, Veteran 7 / 30 / 180 days. Merchant Prince also counts what you pay at the market exchange. Levels already earned stay, and a counter already past a lowered number gets its level at the next login

**NPC lords**
- Their templates build the Banner Hall for their paladin: Hall 1 right after Town Hall 10, Hall 2 with Town Hall 15, Hall 3 with Town Hall 20
- **Lord banners** (world setting *NPC lords use banners*, off by default, needs banners on): lords find banners and standards by the players' drop rules, take conquered ones into their stash, fly the best working banner and standard their personality favours, fuse their least favourite ones, send banner thieves along on retaliations (offensive and balanced lords) and take their paladin along with the standard. Their paladin trains its level in the Banner Hall. While the setting is off, lords behave as in 1.3.2 apart from the hall, and what they already hold sleeps

## For hosts

- **Eight database migrations**, run by Flyway on the first start. They only add tables and columns with defaults, so they take moments:
  - V36: table `one_time_job` (jobs that run once after an update; the paladin's move is the first)
  - V37: table `achievement_grudge` (Avenger)
  - V38: paladin standards: the one-banner-per-village key becomes one per family, the standard a movement carries, and `world_settings.standard_theft_enabled` (true)
  - V39: `world_settings.npc_banners_enabled` (false)
  - V40: table `farm_template` and `farm_target.template_id`
  - V41: the night bonus settings (`night_bonus_enabled` false, 23 to 7, factor 2)
  - V42: table `village_note`
  - V43: table `recruit_template`
- **The paladin's move** (see the heads-up above) runs once, in its own transaction, at the first start; a failed start retries it at the next. Afterwards a Banner Hall that falls to catapults takes nobody's paladin away
- **New world settings** (admin page, world settings card, or `bannerfall-admin world set …`), all changeable in a running world:
  - `npc-banners-enabled` (false): NPC lords use banners and standards
  - `night-bonus-enabled` (false), `night-bonus-start-hour` (23), `night-bonus-end-hour` (7), `night-bonus-factor` (2, 1.1 to 3). The hours are read in the server setting `bannerfall.night-bonus.zone` (default `Europe/Berlin`, so the window follows the clock changes). The battle reads the settings when the attack arrives
  - `standard-theft-enabled` (true): defenders' thieves can take a beaten attack's standard
- The admin page's NPC lords tab shows each lord's paladin level, its villages with a banner or standard and its stash; granting and revoking banners works on lords too
- No new environment variables, no changes to `compose.prod.yaml`, the `Caddyfile` or `ops/`, so `docker compose up -d` only recreates the app
- API: `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /api/farm-templates/{letter}`; farm list entries take and carry `template` (and `note`); `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /api/recruit-templates/{letter}`; `PUT /api/villages/{id}/note`, and `note` on the map's villages; `GET /api/villages/{id}/banner-hall` (Hall training) and `DELETE /api/villages/{villageId}/standard`; research orders take `PALADIN` and `BANNER_THIEF`; battle reports carry `nightBonus` and the standards; `GET /api/world` has `nightBonus`; the simulator takes the standards and `night`; new codes `BANNER_FAMILIES_DIFFER`, `FARM_TEMPLATE_NOT_FOUND`, `RECRUIT_TEMPLATE_NOT_FOUND`

## Upgrading a running world (from v1.3.2)

1. Tell the players about the paladin's move a while ahead: paladins in villages without a Banner Hall are dismissed with a full refund, so whoever wants to keep one builds the hall first (it needs Town Hall 8 and Barracks 5, and works in 1.3.2 already when banners are on)
2. Announce the update (`bannerfall-admin broadcast "Update in 5 minutes"`)
3. Take a backup (`ops/bannerfall-backup`) and check that the file is there
4. `git fetch --tags origin && git checkout v1.4.0`, then either pull the image below or build it yourself with `--build-arg VERSION=1.4.0`; set `BANNERFALL_IMAGE` in `.env` to it and start it (`docker compose -f compose.prod.yaml up -d`)
5. Check the log for the migrations (schema version 43) and the line "Paladin moved into the Banner Hall: N paladin(s) removed", then `bannerfall-admin status` (version 1.4.0) and `bannerfall-admin check`
6. If you want them, switch on the night bonus and lord banners (see *For hosts*)

Going back to 1.3.2 means restoring the backup from step 3 and starting the old image: 1.3.2 knows neither standards nor the paladin in the Banner Hall, and only the backup brings back the paladins the move dismissed. Play since the update is lost.
