**1.7 makes playing together easier and starting out gentler.** A new player is walked through the first steps, guilds can mark their enemies and raise a banner together, villages carry your own tags, your read chats follow you from device to device, and the host's announcements pop up instead of filling the chat. The release builds on 1.6.3; existing worlds keep running and update their database by themselves (migrations V62 to V66).

**Heads-up for running worlds:** the update switches on one thing in every world, the running ones included: the **tutorial** (a "Next step" card for new players; players who already play find it under "Tutorial" in the menu). The host can switch it off on the admin page's World tab. **Guild banners** stay off until the host switches them on. Players choosing a password now get a short refusal for the most common ones.

## What Bannerfall is

A browser strategy game for a small group of friends, in German and English, built for phones first and just as usable on a desktop. One world runs for weeks or for good:

- **Villages:** 16 buildings, from the Town Hall to the Banner Hall, a village picture that grows with them, notes and tags per village, demolishing and automatic building plans
- **Troops:** spearmen, swordsmen, axemen, scouts, light and heavy cavalry, rams, catapults, nobles, the paladin, the banner thief and, optionally, archers and mounted archers; unit levels researched in the Smithy; recruit templates; dismissing units
- **War:** attacks, supports, attack trains, scouting, a wall, the night bonus, loyalty and conquest, morale, an attack simulator and detailed battle reports to share
- **Banners:** found in battle, flown from the Banner Hall, fused, stolen by banner thieves and carried as the paladin's standards
- **Economy:** resources and storage, a market with offers and exchange, merchants between villages, a farm list with templates and "Send all"
- **Players together:** guilds with chat, a political map, enemy marks and an optional guild banner; shared reports; private messages; world chat; achievements; statistics with ranking tabs and charts over time
- **NPC lords and barbarians:** computer lords that build, farm, scout, conquer, form guilds, fly banners and learn from players, so a world with only a few people still has opponents; barbarian villages to farm, with walls
- **How a world ends:** domination, banner keeps or an end date, with a victory clock
- **Comfort:** push notifications while the game is closed, five colour themes in light and dark, medieval or classic icons, a live map with movement lines, a tutorial for new players
- **For hosts:** one Docker image, a guided setup, the admin page and the `bannerfall-admin` console, update and backup scripts, several worlds on one server, and an optional login through an account server shared by several worlds

## What is new

**In-game tutorial**
- A slim **"Next step" card** on the village page leads a new player through nine steps: Woodcutter to level 2, Town Hall to level 3, Barracks, five spearmen, open a barbarian village on the map, send an attack, read its report, open the simulator and put a village on the farm list. The Market and joining a guild are optional steps on the side
- What is done is read from the game, not from clicks. Finishing the Woodcutter, Barracks, spearmen and Market steps **pays back what they cost** once per player, so the first minutes cost nothing. Nothing else is given
- Four short lessons appear when the thing opens: unit levels (Smithy), conquest (Academy), village automation and the Banner Hall. A world that has switched a feature off never shows its lesson
- Everything is skippable: skip a step, skip the whole tutorial, or open the sheet with all steps. New players start it at registration; everybody else finds "Tutorial" in the menu, and starting later ticks off what is done without paying
- The host can switch it off (`tutorial-enabled`)

**Chat read state on every device**
- The chat's "read up to here" marker is kept on the server, per channel (the world chat and your guild's chat), so a message you read on your phone no longer shows as unread on your PC. It only moves forward, and a device that is ahead of the server uploads its state

**Host announcements as popups**
- `bannerfall-admin broadcast` and the admin page now show a **popup with an OK button** to everybody playing, instead of a line in the chat. Several messages share one popup; whoever logs in within ten minutes still sees them, and a dismissed message stays dismissed on that device
- The update scripts warn players this way. `bannerfall-admin chat-say "…"` posts to the chat as `broadcast` did before

**Village tags**
- Give each of your villages a **tag of one to five letters or digits** ("FARM", "WAR2"). It shows in the village switcher, on the village page and in the map panel of your own villages, and the switcher has chips to filter by tag. Tags are private to you; a conquest removes the tag from the village

**Guild enemies**
- A guild's **leader marks enemies**: a guild, a player (also an NPC lord) or a single village, up to 30. Every member sees a red "Enemy" pill by the name in the ranking, on profiles, guild pages and the map panel, a dashed ring on the map and an Enemies card on the guild page. A mark changes no rule; it only shows

**Guild banner** (optional, off by default)
- A guild raises one banner with three levels, paid from a **fund the members donate wood, clay and iron into** (30,000 / 90,000 / 250,000 per member in the guild at the time of the raise). Level 1: **+5 % production** in every member's villages. Level 2: **support to guild mates arrives 10 % sooner**. Level 3: **+5 % defence** for everybody in a member's village. The effects add to those of the item banners
- Any member can raise a level once the fund covers it. For 24 hours after a member leaves or is removed the guild cannot raise, so kicking someone to cut the price doesn't work. NPC lords' guilds have no banner. The simulator and the battle reports show the defence bonus

**Safer accounts**
- A password chosen at registration or when changing it may not be one of the most used ones (a list of about 370, compared without regard to case), the account's own username, or one character repeated; the form says so. Existing passwords, logins and a password reset by the host are not checked
- The login, registration and host-token limits count an IPv6 address by its /64 network, as the account server already did, so one visitor can't get around a limit by changing the end of an address

**Smaller changes**
- The game loads faster: the German and English texts are loaded when needed, which makes the main script about a third smaller. If that download fails, the page retries instead of staying blank
- The player profile's "…" sits beside the buttons, the own guild's page is balanced on desktop, and the broadcast popup focuses OK
- The server logs a warning when an event tick takes more than five seconds or handles an event a minute late

## For hosts

- **Five database migrations**, run by Flyway on the first start. They add tables and two columns with defaults, so they take moments:
  - V62: `chat_seen` (the chat read markers)
  - V63: `village_tag`
  - V64: `guild_enemy`
  - V65: `guild_banner`, `guild_banner_donation` and `world_settings.guild_banners_enabled` (false)
  - V66: `tutorial_state`, `tutorial_progress` and `world_settings.tutorial_enabled` (true)
- **New world settings**, also on the admin page's World tab and in the console (`bannerfall-admin world set …`): `tutorial-enabled` (on) and `guild-banners-enabled` (off; needs guilds)
- **Announcements:** `bannerfall-admin broadcast "…"` is a popup now; use `chat-say "…"` for a chat message. `POST /api/admin/chat` is the same as an API call. The popup messages are kept in the server's memory for ten minutes and are forgotten on a restart
- **Nothing to configure:** no new environment variables, no change to Caddy, `compose.prod.yaml`, `edge/` or the account server. The update scripts only word their warning as a popup
- **API:** `GET` and `PUT /api/chat/seen`; `GET /api/broadcasts`; `PUT /api/villages/{id}/tag` (the tag travels in `/api/me`); `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/guilds/mine/enemies`; `GET /api/guilds/mine/banner` with `/donations` and `/raise`; `POST /api/tutorial/sync`, `/start`, `/stop` and `/steps/{key}/done` and `/skip`; the error `PASSWORD_TOO_COMMON`. Nothing was removed. Details in the API docs
- **Automatic updates:** `ops/bannerfall-auto-update` never installs a new minor version, so a world that uses it moves to 1.7.0 by hand, after reading these notes. From 1.7.0 on it carries on with the patches

## Upgrading a running world (from v1.6.x)

1. `git fetch --tags origin && git checkout v1.7.0`
2. `ops/bannerfall-update 1.7.0` (add `--build` if you build your images on the server). It takes the backup, warns the players with a popup, restarts a minute later, applies V62 to V66 and checks the new version (schema 66). Only the app is recreated
3. Look over the admin page's World tab: the tutorial is on, guild banners are off; switch them as your world wants
4. Several worlds behind the shared Caddy: update every world, then check out the same tag in the edge folder (`edge/` itself is unchanged)

Or by hand, as before: announce the update (`bannerfall-admin broadcast "Update in 5 minutes"`), take a backup (`ops/bannerfall-backup`), pull the image below or build it with `--build-arg VERSION=1.7.0`, set `BANNERFALL_IMAGE` in `.env` to it, `docker compose -f compose.prod.yaml up -d`, then check `bannerfall-admin status` (version 1.7.0) and `bannerfall-admin check`.

Going back to 1.6.3 means restoring the backup taken before the update and starting the old image: the migrations can't be undone, and the play since the update is lost.
