A patch release on top of 1.3.1. NPC lords can form guilds of their own (a world setting, off by default), every world now has a privacy page, the lords farm at a steadier pace and build up their economy earlier, strong scouting shows the troops a village has away, and the top bar warns before the farm is full. It builds on v1.3.1; existing worlds keep running and update their database by themselves (one migration, V35).

## What is new

**Lord guilds** (world setting *NPC lords form guilds*, off by default)
- While the host has them on, NPC lords from 850 points team up with a lord whose village is within 15 fields: they join that lord's guild if it has room, or found one together (at most 3 lords per guild by default, *Most lords per lord guild*). Lord guilds have Latin names and tags from a fixed list (Ordo Corvi `CORVI`, Ordo Leonis `LEO`, …)
- **Guild mates** never attack or scout each other, send support to a mate's village under attack (defensive lords the most, offensive lords none), take the support home once the danger is over, and take over each other's grudges against a player when they are close enough to the attacker. The usual limits stay: all lords together still send at most the day's number of attacks to one player, and none arrives in the quiet hours
- **The brake works per guild:** a lord in a guild stops wanting nobles while its guild's points are more than the lead margin above the strongest human side (a guild of players, or a player without one)
- A lord guild is shown like any guild (ranking, profile, map, guild list and page, with the "NPC lord" mark on its members) and is a victory side like any guild. Lords don't write in the guild chat, never invite players, and players can't invite a lord
- **The admin** sees each lord's guild on the NPC lords tab and can move a lord into another lord guild, take it out, found a new one with it, or let it choose again; `bannerfall-admin npc guild <lord> join <tag> | leave | found | auto` does the same. Switching the setting off ends every lord guild and clears the placements

**Privacy**
- **Privacy page** (`/privacy`, "Datenschutz" in German): what a world stores and why, in German and English: the account (the password only as a hash, no email address), the game, messages and chat, the IP address (in memory only, for a few minutes, for the login, registration and password limits), logs, cookies and browser storage, how long, and the players' rights. Guests reach it under the login and register forms, players from the settings menu
- **Who runs the world:** the page names the host and how to reach them when the host sets `BANNERFALL_OPERATOR_NAME` and `BANNERFALL_OPERATOR_CONTACT` (see *For hosts*). Without them it shows only the data part
- The login limits now forget client addresses within minutes (a sweep every minute) instead of only once 10,000 addresses had piled up

**Scouting**
- **Troops away from the village:** when scouting succeeds and at least 75 % of the attacking scouts survive the scouting phase, the report also shows the owner's own units that are away from the village ("Away from the village"), one total per unit type: supports in other villages and every movement from that village (attacks, supports, units walking home). Not where they are or when they come back. An army that is always out farming can now be spotted by a strong enough scouting
- Reports from before 1.3.2 and the defenders' copies don't have the row

**Village**
- **Farm almost full:** the population in the top bar turns amber while less than 10 % of the farm is free, before it turns red at full

**NPC lords**
- **Farming spread over the day:** a lord's daily farm budget is now spread over the hours it may farm. Within any 2 hours it sends no farm or raid once its loot of those 2 hours reaches budget × 2 ÷ farm hours (an eighth of the budget on Normal), and the loot its attacks still on the way can carry counts too. Before, lords spent the whole day's budget in a few morning hours
- **Mines earlier:** all three templates build the mines to 21/20/20 right after Town Hall 20, before Smithy 20, the Market 10, Warehouse 20 and the Academy. Lords no longer sit at 16/15/15 for days; the first noble comes a little later
- **Losses count:** units a lord loses come off the troop side of its spending ledger, so a lord whose army was wiped out rebuilds it first instead of building on as if it were still there
- **Paladin:** from the second template stage on (850 points), lords recruit one paladin per free paladin slot when the world has paladins. He stays home as a defender

## For hosts

- **One database migration**, run by Flyway on the first start: V35 adds the two lord guild settings to `world_settings` (`npc_guilds_enabled` false, `npc_guild_max_members` 3) and `npc_brain.guild_by_admin` (false). It only adds columns with defaults and takes moments
- **Lord guilds are off after the update.** Turn them on on the admin page (world settings card, *NPC lords form guilds*) or with `bannerfall-admin world set npc-guilds-enabled true` (`npc-guild-max-members` for the size); they need guilds to be on as well. Lords form their guilds in their next rounds
- **New optional `.env` lines** (`.env.example`): `BANNERFALL_OPERATOR_NAME` (the person or club responsible, possibly with a postal address) and `BANNERFALL_OPERATOR_CONTACT` (usually an email address), shown on the privacy page. Left empty, the page explains only what the game stores. The page is a starting point, not legal advice; `docs/hosting.md` says more
- **`Caddyfile`:** a global log filter drops the client address, port and request headers from Caddy's own log lines (it logged them for failed requests, e.g. a 502 while the app restarts). Caddy still keeps no access log. If you run another proxy in front of the app, switch its access log off or tell your players, since the privacy page says no access log is kept
- **`compose.prod.yaml`:** Docker keeps at most three files of 10 MB of console output per container (`x-logging`). Because the logging settings change, the next `docker compose -f compose.prod.yaml up -d` recreates all three containers (app, database and Caddy), not only the app. The data and the certificates stay in their volumes; the site is away for a few seconds longer than usual
- API: `GET /api/world/operator` → `{name, contact}` (both nullable), public and open in the pre-game; scout reports carry `scouting.away` in the attacker's copy when the 75 % rule is met; `PUT /api/admin/npc-lords/{id}/guild` places a lord; `WorldDto` has `npcGuildsEnabled` and `npcGuildMaxMembers`
- Open pages reload by themselves when the server version changes (since 1.2.1)
- The NPC lords' farm loot share (`farm-loot-share`) is still a difficulty setting: a host who wants a different share than the difficulty's sets it on the admin page (difficulty *Custom*)

## Upgrading a running world (from v1.3.1)

1. Announce the update (`bannerfall-admin broadcast "Update in 5 minutes"`)
2. Take a backup (`ops/bannerfall-backup`) and check that the file is there
3. `git fetch --tags origin && git checkout v1.3.2`, then either pull the image below or build it yourself with `--build-arg VERSION=1.3.2`; set `BANNERFALL_IMAGE` in `.env` to it, and add `BANNERFALL_OPERATOR_NAME` and `BANNERFALL_OPERATOR_CONTACT` if you want the privacy page to name you
4. Start it (`docker compose -f compose.prod.yaml up -d`): this time the database and Caddy are recreated as well (new log limits); Caddy reads the new `Caddyfile` on that start
5. Check the log for the migration (schema version 35), then `bannerfall-admin status` (version 1.3.2) and `bannerfall-admin check`, and open `/privacy`
6. If you want lord guilds, turn them on (see *For hosts*)

Going back to 1.3.1 works with the old image and `git checkout v1.3.1` (the old `Caddyfile` and `compose.prod.yaml`), then `up -d` again: 1.3.1 doesn't know the columns of V35 and leaves them alone. If lord guilds were on, switch them off first (that ends every lord guild), since 1.3.1 doesn't expect lords in guilds. The operator lines in `.env` can stay; 1.3.1 ignores them. To undo V35 as well, restore the backup from step 2 (play since the update is lost).
