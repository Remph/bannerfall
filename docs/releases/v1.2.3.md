A patch release for the running playtest. Building levels are worth fewer points at the top, farms give more room in the mid game, a report can go to the whole guild with one tap, the farm list can send just the attacks that can still win a banner, and barbarian reports can be cleared in one go. It builds on v1.2.2; existing worlds keep running and need no database migration.

**Heads-up for running worlds:** on the first start of 1.2.3 the server recalculates the points of every village and player with the new growth, so **everyone's points drop** (a fully built village goes from 69,571 to 29,120). Nobody loses anything else, and achievements already earned stay earned.

## What is new

**Reports**
- **"Send to guild":** the send sheet of a report has a button that sends a copy to every other member of the player's guild, without typing a name. There is no limit of 10, and it is not all or nothing: members a named send would refuse (those the sender blocked, deleted accounts, members with 30 unread copies from the sender) are left out and the rest get it
- **"Delete barbarian reports":** shown in the report list while there are any. After one confirmation with the count, it deletes the player's own attack and scout reports on villages that were barbarian at the time, read or unread. Reports with a "New banner" stay, as do reports other players sent and reports that arrive from now on. Lost attacks are deleted too, and so is the attack report of a conquered barbarian village (the conquest report stays)

**Farm list**
- **"Send banner chances (n)"** next to "Send all": works through the list the same way, but only over the entries that can still win a banner. That means no "Banner chance used" tag, a template that can win a banner (not scouts alone, and at least 30 population against barbarian villages) and no attack of the player's already on the way to that player or barbarian village. A player's villages share one chance, so only the first of them is sent. The count on the button says how many would go; like "Send all" it wants two taps
- The "Banner chance used" tag disappears by itself when the chance is back, and the list refreshes when one of the player's own attacks lands

**Recruiting**
- Each unit shows its total next to what is at home: "60 at home (180 total)". The total counts the village's own units at home and away (supporting elsewhere, on the way out or coming back), the same as the troop overview at the Rally Point; the recruit queue isn't counted

**Balancing: points**
- Each building level is now worth **1.15 ×** the level below it instead of 1.2 (costs grow by about 1.26 per level, so the top levels were worth far too much). Low levels barely change: a new village stays at 39 points, Town Hall 5 goes from 74 to 67. A fully built village is worth 29,120 instead of 69,571, the Town Hall at 30 alone 4,349 instead of 11,817
- The thresholds that depend on points moved to the new scale, at the same build steps as before:

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Growth achievement | 1,000 / 5,000 / 20,000 | 1,000 / 3,500 / 12,500 |
| Village size on the map (medium / large from) | 300 / 2,000 | 280 / 1,600 |
| NPC lord stages (early below / mid below) | 1,000 / 7,000 | 850 / 4,500 |
| Biggest barbarian village an NPC lord probes without scouts | 788 | 591 |

**Balancing: farm**
- The farm gives more population from level 6 on: nothing extra up to level 5, rising to **+15 % at levels 10 to 15**, then down to +3 % from level 22. Buildings take about the same share of the farm at every level; what made farm 10 to 15 tight were the fixed extras (scouts, banner thieves and the paladin). It is never lower than before and works at once, without building anything

| Farm level | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 453 | 1,001 | 2,214 | 4,896 | 10,826 | 23,939 |
| Now | 453 | 1,151 | 2,546 | 5,211 | 11,151 | 24,657 |

## For hosts

- **No database migration** (the schema stays at V30) and nothing to configure: no new settings, no new admin commands, no changes to Caddy, `compose.prod.yaml` or `ops/`
- **Points are recalculated at start:** village points are stored, so every start compares them with the formula and recalculates the villages that differ, together with their owners. The first start of 1.2.3 does this for every village and logs `Points recalculated for N of N villages and their owners`. The game is already open while it runs. Going back to 1.2.2 only needs the old image, but the stored points then stay on the new scale until each village changes; restore the backup for the old numbers
- API: `POST /api/reports/{id}/share-guild` (answers like a named send); `POST /api/reports/delete-barbarian?upTo=` (`204`); the report list carries `barbarian` (how many such reports there are); farm list targets carry `bannerChance`; `POST /api/villages/{id}/farm-targets/send-all?bannerChanceOnly=true` sends only those (`409 BANNERS_DISABLED` without banners or drops)
- Open pages reload by themselves when the server version changes (since 1.2.1)

## Upgrading a running world (from v1.2.2)

1. Announce the update (`bannerfall-admin broadcast "Update in 5 minutes"`), and tell the players their points will drop
2. Take a backup (`ops/bannerfall-backup`) and check that the file is there
3. `git fetch --tags origin && git checkout v1.2.3`, then either pull the image below or build it yourself with `--build-arg VERSION=1.2.3`; set `BANNERFALL_IMAGE` in `.env` to it and start it (`docker compose -f compose.prod.yaml up -d`). Caddy needs no restart
4. Check the log for the points line, then `bannerfall-admin status` (version 1.2.3) and `bannerfall-admin check`
