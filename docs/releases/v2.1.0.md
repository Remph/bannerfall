**2.1 lets you play Bannerfall alone, against NPC lords who can become your allies.** A solo world sleeps while nobody plays, the lords have nine characters, and once they trust you they join your guild, follow your orders and ask you for help. When a world ends, everyone now gets its chronicle: a replay map and the turning points, day by day. The release builds on 2.0.0.

**Heads-up for running worlds:**

- **Seven database migrations, V68 to V74**, run by Flyway on the first start. Take a backup first, as always (`ops/bannerfall-update` takes one):
  - **V68** solo mode, sleep, pause and clock speed in `world_settings` (all off by default)
  - **V69** `npc_contact`: when a player first met a lord. Existing worlds start without contacts, so their lords show no variant until a player meets them again in battle or with a support; lords from before 2.1 keep the classic template
  - **V70** `npc_trust`, `npc_trust_credit`, `private_message.letter` and `world_settings.court_guild_id`: trust starts at 0 for everyone
  - **V71** `npc_order`: orders to allied lords
  - **V72** `npc_help_request` and `npc_trust.warned`: help requests and the warning letter
  - **V73** `chronicle_event` and `chronicle_frame`: the chronicle. The migration copies the conquests from the `CONQUEST` reports that still exist, so an older world's chronicle starts thin; a world that ended before 2.1 gets its end as the only frame
  - **V74** `world_settings.heartbeat_at` (a solo world's last sign of life, so a stopped server doesn't count as game time) and a new default for `npc_trust.warned`
- **Nothing changes in a running world unless the host turns `solo-mode` on.** Invitations, orders and help requests only work in a solo world. Trust is kept in every world with guilds and lord guilds on; outside a solo world the diplomacy page lists only the lords, their guilds and their points
- **A minor version:** `ops/bannerfall-auto-update` will not install it by itself; update by hand (below). The update script of 2.0.0 already checks signed tags, so no by-hand signature check is needed this time. The release key in `ops/release-signers` is unchanged
- **Push notifications:** the new switch "Requests from allies" is on by default

## What is new

**Playing alone**
- **Solo worlds** for one human against NPC lords. The world **falls asleep** two minutes after you close the game and wakes when you open it again: every timer, march and lord picks up where it stopped, so nothing happens behind your back. A stopped or suspended server doesn't count as game time either
- **The host's world clock:** pause, play, normal, and fast ×2 or ×4, on the admin page's World tab or with `bannerfall-admin world clock`. A paused world shows a strip ("world paused") and waits until the host presses Play
- **The Solo preset** in the guided setup: speed 4, eight lords in lord guilds of up to three, guilds on, no night bonus, a domination win at 60 % held for 48 hours. In a solo world the lords keep no quiet hours. The preset makes a fresh invite code instead of the default one. The difficulty levels (Easy, Normal, Hard, Custom) now carry a name and one sentence each
- **On your own PC with one command:** `ops/bannerfall-solo` starts the game and its database with Docker (`compose.solo.yaml`, no domain, no Caddy, the game at `http://localhost:8461`), writes its own secrets and prints the setup link. `stop`, `update` (with a backup first), `backup`, `restore FILE`, `reset` and `admin` do the rest. The solo server answers only on `localhost`, so a web page can't reach it through a borrowed name (DNS rebinding)

**Allies**
- **Trust:** every lord keeps a trust value towards every player, from −100 to 100, drifting back towards 0. A support that stands during an attack, shipments and a win against a lord's old enemy raise it; attacks, scouting, attacks on its guild mates and conquests lower it
- **Invitations:** in a solo world a guild leader can invite a lord. It answers in its next round and says yes once its trust is high enough (30, 50 or 70 by personality, more while it is in a lord guild) and the guild has room. How high that bar is shows only once you have met the lord. A lord that is already another player's ally can't be invited. An ally leaves when its trust in the guild's leader falls below 0, and at once when its own guild attacks it. A leader whose other members are all allies can leave, and the guild ends
- **The court:** the strongest all-lord guild is the court ("The Raven Court"), named wherever a victory side is named. Everyone hears when another guild becomes the court
- **The diplomacy page** (`/diplomacy`, under Player): the court and its race against you, your allies and the other lords with their trust, one Invite per row, and the open orders. While the world is paused or has ended, the buttons to invite and order are hidden
- **Orders:** from a village's map panel or the diplomacy page, order your allies to attack, support, send resources or hold off a village. An attack can **land together** with your own, 30 seconds after it. The lords decide for themselves: they refuse targets that are too strong or protected, scout first when they know nothing, and answer by letter
- **Help requests:** an ally asks for defenders when an attack it can't hold is on the way, or for resources when a shortage holds back its noble or its wall. A strip under the sky counts down; one tap opens the rally point or the market filled in, from the nearest village whose fastest defenders arrive in time (or that has a free merchant), and the sheet says so when nothing can arrive in time. Help in time earns trust and a thank-you shipment; "Not this time" and silence cost trust, except for defenders that could never arrive in time
- **Letters in a voice:** the lords write in three voices (careful, steady, bold) in German and English: answers, thanks, a warning when trust falls below 25 (before an ally starts refusing orders), and the court's taunt when it starts the victory clock

**NPC lords**
- **Nine variants** on the three personalities: Robber Knight, Conqueror and Hothead (offensive); Castellan, Protector and Merchant Lord (defensive); Settler, Knight of Honour and Vulture (balanced). They raid, hold grudges, support, ship and pick targets in their own ways. A lord's page names its variant once you have met it in battle or with a support. The host can spawn a chosen variant (`bannerfall-admin npc spawn <personality> [variant|plain]`)
- **No more frozen lords:** a lord's village rebuilds what it lost before it saves for nobles, and saves for nobles only with an army to escort them

**The chronicle**
- When a world ends, its story is on the end screen and stays on the page `/chronicle` (linked from the ranking's victory card): a **replay map** of who held what, day by day (play, step, slider), and a **timeline** of the turning points: guilds founded and disbanded, allies, the court, the victory clock, the end, the conquests that matter, and a new leader in points. Tap a moment and the map jumps to that day

**Look and fixes**
- The Now list shows other players' merchants on their way to your village
- The march bars on the rally point and in the Now list take the colours of the map (attack, support, return), readable in every theme by day and at night
- Long village names are no longer cut in the header; name notes on the ranking and the profiles wrap flush
- Unread counts for messages and chat are no longer red (red stays for reports and attacks); coordinates no longer break across lines in Firefox; row buttons stay in one place
- The notification switches list only what the world can send (no "Requests from allies" outside a solo world)
- More render tests (farm list, achievements, notifications, simulator); checked in Firefox and WebKit as well as Chromium

**For hosts**
- **API:** new `GET`/`PUT /api/admin/world-clock`, `GET /api/chronicle`, `GET /api/diplomacy` with invitations, orders and help requests; `GET /api/players/{id}` gains `lordVariant`. Nothing was removed. New errors: `WORLD_PAUSED`, `WORLD_NOT_SOLO`, `LORD_NOT_READY`, `LORD_ALLIED`, `NOT_AN_ALLY`, `ORDER_TARGET_INVALID`, `HELP_REQUEST_CLOSED`; `GET /api/chronicle` answers `WORLD_NOT_ENDED` before the end
- **New `bannerfall-admin` commands:** `world clock [pause|play|normal|fast2|fast4]`, `world set solo-mode true`, and the variant on `npc spawn`
- **Account server:** the career page formats the village count; rebuild its image when you run one (optional for the game itself)
- **Host check:** when `BANNERFALL_DOMAIN` carries a scheme (as on the solo world, `http://localhost:8461`), the server answers only that host and the loopback names, and every other host gets 421. Worlds behind Caddy (a domain without a scheme) are not affected. `.env.*` files stay out of the image build
- **Nothing else to configure:** no new required environment variables, no change to Caddy, `compose.prod.yaml` or `edge/`
- The README now starts with playing; *Deploying* is called *Hosting a world*

## Upgrading a running world (from v2.0.0)

1. `cd /opt/bannerfall && git fetch --tags origin`
2. `ops/bannerfall-update 2.1.0` (add `--build` if you build your images on the server). It checks the tag's signature against `ops/release-signers`, takes the backup, warns the players with a popup, restarts a minute later, applies V68 to V74 and checks the new version
3. Several worlds behind the shared Caddy: update every world, then check out the same tag in the edge folder (`edge/` itself is unchanged)
4. To make an existing world a solo world: `bannerfall-admin world set solo-mode true`. It is meant for one human and the lords; a friend can still join, and the world then sleeps only while nobody is playing

Or by hand, as before: announce the update (`bannerfall-admin broadcast "Update in 5 minutes"`), take a backup (`ops/bannerfall-backup`), pull the image below or build it with `--build-arg VERSION=2.1.0`, set `BANNERFALL_IMAGE` in `.env` to it, `docker compose -f compose.prod.yaml up -d`, then check `bannerfall-admin status` (version 2.1.0) and `bannerfall-admin check`.

Coming from 1.7.x: update to 2.0.0 first (its notes describe the one signature check by hand), then to 2.1.0.

Going back to 2.0.0 means restoring the backup taken before the update and starting the old image: the migrations can't be undone, and the play since the update is lost.

## Known limits

- The trust, order and help numbers (gains and losses, the invitation bars, the request limits) are first values from the simulations and may be tuned once solo worlds see real play
- Still placeholders, as in 2.0: the paladin's and the standards' numbers, the unit level values, the banner numbers and the archers' numbers; the guild banner's prices and effects, morale, the noble price and the achievement numbers
